ABOUT PUSHKAR

A Sacred Town

Pushkar is one of Rajasthan's most famous spiritual destinations. The town surrounds the beautiful Pushkar Lake and is known for its peaceful atmosphere.

With its temples, colourful markets and traditional culture, Pushkar offers a unique experience for travellers.

MUST VISIT

Famous Attractions

Pushkar Lake

A sacred lake surrounded by temples and ghats where visitors can experience the peaceful spiritual atmosphere of Pushkar.

Brahma Temple

One of the most famous temples in Pushkar, dedicated to Lord Brahma and an important part of the town's spiritual heritage.

Pushkar Market

Explore colourful streets filled with handicrafts, traditional clothes, jewellery, souvenirs and local products.
