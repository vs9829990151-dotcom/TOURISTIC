ABOUT UDAIPUR

The City of Lakes

Udaipur is one of Rajasthan's most beautiful destinations. The city is surrounded by the Aravalli Hills and is known for its lakes, palaces and historic architecture.

Its peaceful atmosphere, colourful streets and royal heritage make Udaipur a popular destination for travellers.

MUST VISIT

Famous Attractions

City Palace

A magnificent palace complex overlooking Lake Pichola, known for its architecture, courtyards and royal history.

Lake Pichola

Enjoy peaceful boat rides and beautiful views of the surrounding palaces and hills.

Monsoon Palace

A hilltop palace offering panoramic views of Udaipur and its surrounding landscape.

TRAVEL GUIDE

Udaipur at a Glance

🚤 Experience

Take a boat ride on Lake Pichola and enjoy the beautiful views around the lake.

🍛 Food

Try traditional Rajasthani thali, Dal Baati Churma and local snacks.

📸 Best Views

Explore lakeside areas, old-city streets and hilltop viewpoints around the city.
