ABOUT MOUNT ABU

Rajasthan's Hill Escape

Mount Abu is the only major hill station in Rajasthan. Located in the Aravalli Range, it offers a refreshing contrast to the desert landscapes of the state.

Its green surroundings, peaceful lakes, ancient temples and scenic viewpoints make it a popular destination for travellers.

MUST VISIT

Famous Attractions

Nakki Lake

A beautiful lake surrounded by hills, gardens and viewpoints, perfect for a peaceful evening and boating experience.

Dilwara Temples

A remarkable group of Jain temples known for their detailed marble architecture and impressive craftsmanship.

Guru Shikhar

The highest point in Rajasthan, offering panoramic views of the Aravalli mountains and surrounding landscapes.
