ABOUT JAISALMER

The Golden City

Jaisalmer is located in the heart of the Thar Desert and is known for its beautiful golden sandstone buildings.

The city's historic fort, colourful markets and vast desert landscapes make it one of the most unique destinations in Rajasthan.

MUST VISIT

Famous Attractions

Jaisalmer Fort

A magnificent golden sandstone fort rising from the desert and home to historic streets, temples and shops.

Sam Sand Dunes

Enjoy camel rides, desert sunsets, cultural performances and a memorable night under the desert sky.

Patwon Ki Haveli

Explore beautifully decorated historic havelis showcasing traditional architecture and artistic craftsmanship.

TRAVEL GUIDE

Jaisalmer at a Glance

🐪 Desert

Experience camel rides, jeep safaris and beautiful sunset views across the Thar Desert.

🍛 Food

Enjoy traditional Rajasthani dishes, Dal Baati Churma and local desert snacks.

🛍️ Shopping

Shop for traditional handicrafts, colourful textiles, leather products and souvenirs.
