ABOUT JODHPUR

The Blue City

Jodhpur is one of Rajasthan's most fascinating cities. The old city is known for its blue houses and narrow colourful streets.

Dominating the city skyline is Mehrangarh Fort, one of the most impressive historic forts in Rajasthan.

MUST VISIT

Famous Attractions

Mehrangarh Fort

A massive hilltop fort offering spectacular views over Jodhpur and containing beautiful historic courtyards and museums.

Blue City

Walk through the old city and discover its famous blue-painted houses, local shops and traditional streets.

Jaswant Thada

A beautiful marble memorial surrounded by peaceful gardens and offering views of the city and Mehrangarh Fort.

TRAVEL GUIDE

Jodhpur at a Glance

🍛 Food

Try famous Jodhpuri dishes, Mirchi Vada, Makhaniya Lassi and traditional Rajasthani food.

🛍️ Shopping

Explore local markets for handicrafts, textiles, spices, jewellery and souvenirs.

📸 Experience

Explore the old blue city, visit Mehrangarh Fort and enjoy panoramic sunset views.
