ABOUT JAIPUR

Where Royal History Comes Alive

Jaipur is popularly known as the Pink City because of the distinctive colour of many buildings in the old city.

The city is famous for its magnificent forts, royal palaces, colourful bazaars, traditional handicrafts and delicious Rajasthani cuisine.

MUST VISIT

Famous Attractions

Amber Fort

A magnificent hilltop fort known for its grand architecture, courtyards and beautiful views of the surrounding landscape.

Hawa Mahal

One of Jaipur's most recognisable landmarks, famous for its unique five-storey facade and hundreds of small windows.

City Palace

A beautiful royal complex featuring courtyards, museums, decorated halls and historic architecture.

TRAVEL GUIDE

Jaipur at a Glance

🍛 Food

Try Dal Baati Churma, Ghewar, Pyaaz Kachori and traditional Rajasthani thali.

🛍️ Shopping

Explore Johari Bazaar and Bapu Bazaar for jewellery, textiles, handicrafts and souvenirs.

📸 Experience

Enjoy the old city, local markets, historic architecture and beautiful sunset views.
